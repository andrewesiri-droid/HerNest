HerNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

HerNest collects information you provide: your name, family details, wellness data, budget information, and conversation history with Nora. We also collect your Google account information when you sign in.

How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to personalise Nora's responses and improve your experience. We never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models. Each user's data is completely isolated.

AI conversations

When you chat with Nora, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API via our secure server proxy. We do not store your conversations beyond what you see in the app. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to message processing.

Financial data

Budget data and expense history are stored in your private Firebase account. We never connect to your bank directly. We never see your bank credentials.

Data storage & security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Firebase encryption). Firebase security rules ensure no user can access another user's data.

Your rights

You have the right to access, export, and delete all your data at any time. Use the "Download my data" and "Delete my account" options in the app, or contact us at privacy@hernest.app.

Cookies & analytics

We use PostHog for anonymous usage analytics to improve the product. We do not use advertising cookies.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@hernest.app
